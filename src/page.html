How to play and scoring

The table

  • You sit South. North is your partner; East and West are the opponents. All three are bots.
  • Each deal is 13 cards a player. Dealer and vulnerability rotate with the board number.

Claiming

  • When the rest of the tricks are clear, tap Claim during the play and say how many your side takes. East and West accept if those tricks are there with best play; otherwise play goes on. Claiming none concedes the rest.

Bidding

  • Calls go clockwise from the dealer. A bid names a level (1 to 7) and a strain; each bid must outrank the last. Strains rank clubs, diamonds, hearts, spades, notrump.
  • You may double an opponent's bid and redouble their double. Three passes in a row after a bid end the auction; four passes at the start throw the deal in.
  • The last bid is the contract: level + 6 tricks with that suit as trumps. Declarer is the first player of that side who named the strain.
  • Tap any call in the auction to see what it shows.

Play

  • The player on declarer's left leads, then dummy's hand goes face up. Follow suit if you can; otherwise play anything.
  • The highest card of the suit led wins the trick unless a trump is played, in which case the highest trump wins. The winner leads next.
  • When your side declares you play both your own hand and North's.
  • Press a card to lift it, slide to change your mind, release to play it.

Scoring (duplicate)

  • Tricks bid and made: clubs and diamonds 20 each, hearts and spades 30, notrump 40 for the first and 30 after. Doubled counts twice, redoubled four times.
  • 100 or more trick points is a game: +300, or +500 vulnerable. Anything less is a part-score: +50.
  • Small slam (12 tricks bid): +500, or +750 vulnerable. Grand slam (13): +1000, or +1500.
  • Overtricks score their trick value; doubled they are 100 each (200 vulnerable), redoubled twice that. Making a doubled contract adds 50, redoubled 100.
  • Undertricks cost 50 each, 100 vulnerable. Doubled: 100, 300, 500, then 300 more each; vulnerable 200, 500, 800, then 300 more each.
  • WE on the score pad is North-South, THEY is East-West.
